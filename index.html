Remote Work Visa requirements: Employment contract (1 year+), employer letter, latest payslip, 3 months bank statements.

Step 2 — Prepare Key Documents

  • Passport valid for at least 6 months
  • Police clearance certificate (attested)
  • Attested university degrees or certificates
  • Attested marriage and birth certificates (for family sponsorship)

Step 3 — Visa Processing

Business setup clients start the company formation process first, after which we begin working on the visa. Direct visa clients proceed immediately to visa processing.

Step 4 — In-Country Legal Process (7–10 working days)

  • Medical Fitness Test: Mandatory blood test and chest X-ray
  • Biometrics & Emirates ID: Fingerprints and eye scans at a government centre
  • Visa Stamping & Activation: Residency visa linked to your passport

Step 5 — Settling Arrival Logistics

  • Banking: Open a personal bank account with passport, Emirates ID and salary certificate
  • Housing (Ejari): Register your lease through the Ejari system. Landlords may request 1–4 post-dated cheques for the year
  • Utilities (DEWA): Activate electricity and water using your Ejari
  • Family Sponsorship: Initiate dependent visa processes once your own visa is stamped

No Personal Income Tax

The UAE does not impose personal income tax on UAE nationals or foreigners. As an expat, you will not be taxed on:

  • Employment income
  • Rental income
  • Dividends, royalties and capital gains
  • Freelance and self-employment income
  • Inheritance and gifts

Corporate Tax

9% on business profits above AED 375,000. Freelancers and self-employed expats are subject to corporate tax only if their combined turnover exceeds AED 1 million annually.

VAT

5% on most goods and services in Dubai.

Double Tax Treaties

The UAE has double taxation agreements with 100+ countries, including the UK and Australia. You qualify as a UAE tax resident if you meet one of these criteria:

  • Your primary residence and centre of financial interests are in the UAE
  • You are physically present in the UAE for at least 183 days within 12 months

Sponsorship Requirements

  • Minimum monthly salary of AED 4,000 (or AED 3,000 plus accommodation)
  • Attested birth and marriage certificates
  • Family members over 18 must pass medical fitness tests
  • 60 days from arrival to apply for family residence visas

Sponsorship Rules

  • You can sponsor your daughters regardless of age (as long as they are unmarried)
  • You can sponsor your sons until they turn 25
  • Parents can be sponsored on an annual basis
